How to Play

Reach the portal. Do the objectives first.

Move

WASD run relative to the camera.

Mouse orbits the camera. Wheel zooms. ←→ also turn it.

Jump & dash

Space jump, press again in the air for a second jump.

Shift air-dash in the direction you are moving. One per airtime.

Objectives

◆ collect every crystal · ▣ press every switch · ◎ then enter the portal.

Checkpoint flags save your respawn point. Falling hurts nothing but your time.

Hazards

Spikes, spinning bars and lasers kill instantly. Crumbling tiles fall away.

Green pads launch you skyward. Dark belts shove you around.

Keys

R restart · Esc pause · M mute · F toggle fullscreen.

Controller

Left stick or d-pad moves · A jump (twice for a double jump) · X/RB/RT dash.

Right stick orbits the camera · Menu pauses and resumes.

In menus, the d-pad or left stick moves the highlight, A selects and B goes back.

Jump, land, dash and wipeout buzz the pad. Switch that off with Rumble in the pause menu.

Stars

★★★ beat par time without dying. ★★ fast or careful. ★ for finishing.

Progress saves automatically in this browser.

RUN PAUSED

Controller disconnected

The controller dropped out, so the run is paused. Reconnect it to carry on from where you stopped, or keep going on the keyboard.
